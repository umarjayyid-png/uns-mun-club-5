01 / ABOUT US

MORE THAN A SIMULATION

Learn to engage
with the world.

UNS Model United Nations Club is a student organization dedicated to developing diplomatic skills, critical thinking, global awareness, and professional confidence.

Through discussions, professional classes, delegation preparation, and conference experiences, members are encouraged to research global issues, communicate clearly, negotiate thoughtfully, and collaborate across perspectives.
